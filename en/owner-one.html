Owner.One

Overview

Designed the MVP of Owner.One, an international platform for managing asset data and passing assets on to heirs. Worked on the product logic and the app interfaces. The product later became a finalist at the Fintech Finance Awards 2025 (Wealth) and the Wealth Management Industry Awards 2026 (Technology), and won the Global Recognition Award 2026 for innovation.
