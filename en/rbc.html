App Store

The idea for updating RBC’s flagship app came from designing the tvOS version, which brings the company’s media channels together. The interface was refined through UX testing of reading and viewing scenarios.

RBC Pro

Designed the paywall, onboarding, reader, and B2C/B2B accounts. Of 300K+ registered users, more than 50K became paying subscribers – a 16.6% conversion rate. Readers viewed 3.8 pages per visit, and sessions lasted up to 6 minutes.

Designed interfaces for RBC Trends (3M MAU), the RBC Biztorg catalog of franchises and ready‑made businesses (12,000+ listings), digital special issues, and the RBC+ apps.
